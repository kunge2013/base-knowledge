## Semantic search 语义搜索

Semantic search is a way to search for text based on the inherent meaning or context, rather than relying solely on keywords or other traditional search methods.  
语义搜索是一种基于文本的内在含义或上下文来进行搜索的方法，而不是仅仅依赖于关键词或其他传统的搜索方式。

Semantic search is accomplished using AI models to transform the text into vectors, which are arrays of numbers, and are called "embeddings". If the model is effective, the vectors, each of size N, that are close to each other in N-dimensional space are the ones that have similar underlying or semantic meaning. For example, the embedding vector of “face mask” will be closer to the embedding vector of “face covering” than it is to “respirator.”  
语义搜索是通过使用人工智能模型将文本转化为向量来实现的。这些向量实际上是一组数字，被称为“嵌入向量”。如果模型运行有效，那么在 N 维空间中彼此相距较近的这些向量，就代表了相似或相同的语义含义。例如， “口罩” 的嵌入向量会与 “面部覆盖物” 的嵌入向量更为接近，而会与 “呼吸器” 的嵌入向量相距较远。

![Embeddings visualization.](https://www.palantir.com/docs/resources/foundry/ontology/aip-embeddings-visualization.png)

If the embedded text is then associated with a particular object in the [Ontology](https://www.palantir.com/docs/foundry/ontology/overview/), then your search-driven operational workflows become much more useful. Finding related entities or entities related to a particular search query is simply finding the nearest vectors in N-dimensional space.  
如果嵌入的文本与本体中的某个特定对象相关联，那么基于搜索的操作流程就会变得更加有用。找到与特定搜索查询相关的实体，实际上就是在 N 维空间中找到最近的向量。
