## Authentication 认证

There are two options for authentication on the Palantir platform. Enrollment administrators can integrate an existing identity provider, or you can use Palantir’s self-service passwordless identity provider for AIP Developer Tier and AIP Bootcamp enrollments.  
在 Palantir 平台上，有两种认证方式可供选择。管理员可以选择集成现有的身份提供者，或者使用 Palantir 提供的无需密码的身份认证服务，适用于 AIP 开发者级别和 AIP 培训课程的注册流程。

[Learn more about logging in to a Palantir enrollment.  
了解更多关于如何登录 Palantir 注册账户的信息。](https://www.palantir.com/docs/foundry/getting-started/login/)

## Your own identity provider您的自有身份认证提供商

The Palantir platform can integrate seamlessly with your existing identity provider, allowing full end-to-end access administration and management through your existing system. Review the [administration documentation](https://www.palantir.com/docs/foundry/authentication/overview/) for detailed instructions on how to configure your identity provider for use with the Palantir platform.  
Palantir 平台可以与您现有的身份提供商无缝集成，使得能够通过您现有的系统来实现完整的端到端访问管理功能。有关如何配置身份提供商以与 Palantir 平台协同使用的详细说明，请参考相关管理文档。

## Palantir self-service user directoryPalantir 自助服务用户目录

In some scenarios, your enrollment may be automatically configured with a built-in identity provider. Palantir's self-service user directory is **passwordless**, leveraging FIDO2 passkeys to offer unparalleled security and a seamless user experience.  
在某些情况下，您的注册可能会自动配置一个内置的身份验证提供商。Palantir 的自助服务用户目录采用无密码认证方式，利用 FIDO2 生物密钥提供无与伦比的安全性和无缝的用户体验。

If you signed up for a new enrollment with the Palantir self-service user directory, you will receive an email with the subject "Set up your Palantir account and log in" shortly after signing up. After [completing the instructions to set up your account](https://www.palantir.com/docs/foundry/getting-started/login/#set-up-and-configure-a-passkey), you can invite additional users to your enrollment in Control Panel by navigating to **Authentication > Palantir self-service user directory**. Then, select **Manage users**.  
如果您是通过 Palantir 自助用户目录进行新注册申请的，那么在注册后不久，您将会收到一封主题为“设置您的 Palantir 账户并登录”的邮件。完成账户设置的步骤后，您可以进入“认证”>“Palantir 自助用户目录”页面，然后选择“管理用户”，从而将其他用户添加到您的注册列表中。

We recommend that you configure more than one [enrollment administrator](https://www.palantir.com/docs/foundry/administration/enrollments-and-organizations-permissions/) to assist with account recovery as a backup.  
我们建议您配置多个注册管理员，以便作为备用方案来协助账户恢复工作。

### What are passkeys? 什么是密码密钥？

FIDO2 (Fast IDentity Online) passkeys are a passwordless login method that use cryptography and a device's built-in security features, such as facial recognition, to verify your identity. A passkey is not the same as a password, and you do not need to remember it or enter it into a login form. Instead, your device will verify your identity using security features such as biometrics, and provide a private cryptographic key to authenticate you, allowing you to log in. Passkeys eliminate the need to remember complex passwords and allow you to sign in with your fingerprint, face scan, security key, or password manager.  
FIDO2（快速身份在线认证）的密码密钥是一种无需密码的登录方式，它利用密码学技术和设备的内置安全功能，如面部识别，来验证您的身份。密码密钥与常规密码不同，您无需记住它或将其输入到登录表单中。相反，您的设备会利用生物识别等安全功能来验证您的身份，并提供一个私密的密码密钥以进行身份验证，从而让您能够成功登录。这种方式消除了记住复杂密码的需求，同时允许您使用指纹、面部扫描、安全密钥或密码管理器等方式进行登录。

### How do passkeys work?物理键盘的密码如何工作呢？

A FIDO2 passkey is a physical security key or a platform authenticator, such as a biometric device or a smartphone, that can be used for passwordless authentication. Devices such as smartphones generate a unique pair of public and private keys for each service or application. The public key is registered with the service, and the private key remains securely stored on the device.  
FIDO2 密钥是一种物理安全密钥或平台认证工具，可以是生物识别设备或智能手机等。这种密钥可以用于无需密码的认证。像智能手机这样的设备会为每个服务或应用程序生成一对唯一的公钥和私钥。公钥会被注册到相关服务中，而私钥则安全地存储在设备内部。

When you use a FIDO2 passkey for authentication, the service will send a challenge to your device. The device will sign the challenge using the private key and send the signed response back to the service. The service then verifies the response using the public key to confirm your identity.  
当你使用 FIDO2 密码键盘进行身份验证时，服务会向你的设备发送一个挑战码。你的设备会使用私钥对挑战码进行签名，然后将签名的响应返回给服务。服务会使用公钥来验证该响应，从而确认你的身份。

Passkeys provide several benefits:  
密码钥匙具有多种优势：

- **Strong security:** Public-key cryptography provides a high level of security, and since the private key never leaves the device, it is less vulnerable to attacks.  
	强大的安全性：公钥密码学提供了高度的安全性，而且由于私钥永远不会离开设备，因此也更容易抵御攻击。
- **Passwordless authentication:** FIDO2 passkeys eliminate the need for passwords, making authentication more convenient and reducing the risk of phishing and other password-related attacks.  
	无需密码的认证：FIDO2 密钥卡消除了对密码的需求，使得认证过程更加便捷，同时降低了遭受网络钓鱼和其他密码相关攻击的风险。
- **Privacy:** The unique key pairs generated for each service ensure that your authentication information cannot be used to track your activities across different services.  
	隐私保护：为每项服务生成的唯一密钥对，确保您的认证信息不会被用于追踪您在不同服务中的活动。
- **Ease of use:** Passkeys provide a simple, user-friendly authentication experience that requires only a single action, such as inserting the security key or using a biometric device such as a fingerprint or face scan.  
	易用性：指纹钥匙的认证过程非常简单且用户友好，只需要进行一次操作即可，比如插入安全钥匙或使用指纹识别、面部识别等生物识别技术。

For detailed instructions on logging in with a passkey, review the [login documentation](https://www.palantir.com/docs/foundry/getting-started/login/).  
如需了解使用密码进行登录的详细步骤，请参考登录文档。
