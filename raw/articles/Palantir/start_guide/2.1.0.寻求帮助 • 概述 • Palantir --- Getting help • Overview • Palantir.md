## Help and support 帮助和支持

If you encounter any issues while using the Palantir platform, you can review guidance on how to investigate, diagnose, and find a suitable resolution below.  
如果您在使用 Palantir 平台时遇到任何问题，您可以参考以下指南来调查、诊断并找到合适的解决方案。

## 1\. Investigate the issue1. 调查这个问题

Before submitting a support request, follow the steps below to address a potential issue. By collecting the information outlined below, the investigation process can be expedited and help us to provide an optimal resolution.  
在提交支持请求之前，请按照以下步骤来处理潜在的问题。通过收集以下列出的信息，可以加快调查进程，从而帮助我们提供最佳的解决方案。

1. Start by reviewing error messages from the platform and compile them into a document.  
	首先，请回顾平台给出的错误信息，并将这些信息整理成一份文档。
2. If an error is not clear or does not provide actionable next steps, use [Chrome™ Developer tools](https://www.palantir.com/docs/foundry/getting-help/debug-using-devtools/) to gather more information on the issue.  
	如果某个错误并不明确，或者没有提供可行的解决步骤，可以使用 Chrome™开发者工具来收集更多关于该问题的信息。
3. Consider whether any changes were made since the platform was last behaving as expected. Try reverting these changes and test if the issue is resolved.  
	请考虑自该平台上次表现符合预期之后，是否发生了任何变化。尝试回退这些更改，然后测试问题是否得到解决。
4. Check status emails, alerts, and announcements for known issues or planned maintenance that might affect your work.  
	请检查状态邮件、警报以及公告，了解可能影响您工作的已知问题或计划中的维护工作。
5. Search the [platform documentation](https://www.palantir.com/docs/foundry/) to see if there are useful suggestions.  
	请查阅平台的文档，看看是否有有用的建议可以参考。
6. Copy the errorID/errorInstanceID and share it with support.  
	请复制“errorID/errorInstanceID”字段，并将其告知技术支持人员。
7. Use Data Lineage to investigate issues with your pipeline (for example, permissions, build status, build schedules, etc.)  
	使用数据溯源功能来排查管道中的各种问题（例如权限、构建状态、构建计划等）。
	- Selecting different node coloring options (For example, `Time last built` or `Permissions`) allows you to highlight different characteristics of the resources in a pipeline.  
		选择不同的节点着色选项（例如 `Time last built` 或 `Permissions` ），可以突出显示流水线中资源的各种特性。

## 2\. Search the Issues application and the Developer Community2. 搜索“Issues”应用程序和开发者社区中的相关资源。

With the additional information you have obtained from investigating on your own, you can visit:  
通过你自己调查所获得的其他信息，你可以访问：

- The **Issues** application to see issues reported by other users which may help resolve or provide insight into your issue. This application provides transparency into questions in the platform by tracking past questions, providing previous engagement from Palantir Support, and a timeline on issue resolution.  
	“查看其他用户报告的问题”功能可以帮助您解决遇到的问题，或者获得有关问题的更多信息。该功能通过跟踪以往的问题记录，提供 Palantir 支持方面的过往回应，并明确问题解决的期限，从而让平台上的问题得到更清晰的呈现。

![Issues app index displaying a list of sample issues](https://www.palantir.com/docs/resources/foundry/getting-help/issues-app.png)

- The [Palantir Developer Community ↗](https://community.palantir.com/) to ask and answer questions from your fellow users. Here, you will find questions asked by other Palantir users and get help on the issue you are facing.  
	Palantir 开发者社区是一个供用户提问和解答问题的平台。在这里，你可以看到其他 Palantir 用户提出的问题，并获得关于你所面临的问题的帮助。

## Further assistance 进一步的帮助

In cases where you are not able to find a solution either from resources above or our [platform documentation](https://www.palantir.com/docs/foundry/), learn how to [prepare and file a support ticket](https://www.palantir.com/docs/foundry/getting-help/file-support-ticket/) which details how to collect debugging information and create a similar example so that Palantir Support is armed to provide a quick resolution to your issue.  
如果您无法从上述资源或我们的平台文档中找到解决方案，请学习如何准备并提交支持请求。在请求中详细说明如何收集调试信息以及创建类似的示例，这样 Palantir 的支持团队就能更快地为您解决问题。
