## Start with Training and Palantir Learning portal从培训和 Palantir 学习平台开始吧。

The Training application presents you a curated set of courses from [the Palantir Learning portal at learn.palantir.com ↗](https://learn.palantir.com/), and links to all in-platform and public material for learning how to use the Palantir platform.  
培训应用程序提供了从 Palantir 学习平台精选出的课程列表，这些课程可以在 learn.palantir.com 上查看。此外，该应用程序还链接到了平台内的所有学习资料以及公开可用的资源，帮助用户了解如何正确使用 Palantir 平台。

![Training application](https://www.palantir.com/docs/resources/foundry/getting-started/training-application.png?width=800)

We also recommend a visit to [learn.palantir.com ↗](https://learn.palantir.com/) for a variety of courses that can help you familiarize with the platform and in its usage.  
我们还推荐您访问 learn.palantir.com 网站，那里有各种课程可以帮助您熟悉该平台及其使用方法。

## Surface training documents for your organization适用于您组织的地面训练文档

The Training application allows you to surface Notepad documents with information about trainings relevant for your organization.  
该培训应用程序允许您查看包含与您组织相关的培训信息的 Notepad 文档。

To surface Notepad documents with training material and instructions, [create a tag category](https://www.palantir.com/docs/foundry/compass/tags/) named `Training application`. Every Notepad document that is accessible by a user and tagged with a tag under the tag category `Training application` will be shown in the training application. You can freely name tags within the category which will be shown in-line to the link to the document.  
要显示包含培训材料和说明的 Notepad 文档，请创建一个名为 `Training application` 的标签类别。任何由用户可访问且带有 `Training application` 类别下某个标签的 Notepad 文档，都会出现在培训应用程序中。你可以自由为这个类别中的标签命名，这些标签会直接显示在文档链接旁边。
